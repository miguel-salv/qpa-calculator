CMU QPA Calculator

Enter your courses and grades to calculate your Quality Point Average. Everything runs in your browser, so your academic records never leave your device.

QPA = total quality points ÷ total factorable units. Quality points = units × grade value (A=4, B=3, C=2, D=1, R=0). Pass/No-Grade, W, I, and audit grades don’t count.
